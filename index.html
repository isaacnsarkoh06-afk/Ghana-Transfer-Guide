HELP CENTRE

Frequently asked questions

Important details for sending money internationally.

How can I send money to Ghana from another country?

Select your sending country, review providers, then follow the chosen provider's official process. Confirm the destination and payout method before paying.

Can I send money directly to MTN MoMo?

Some providers and routes may offer mobile-wallet payouts, but availability depends on your country, provider rules, and recipient eligibility. Confirm MTN Mobile Money is available before proceeding.

Which transfer services can I use?

This directory links to WorldRemit, Remitly, Western Union, and MoneyGram. Their presence here does not mean every service supports your route or MTN MoMo payout.

Can I send money from my country?

Not necessarily. Services operate in selected sending countries. Choose your country in the finder and verify the route on the provider's official website.

How do I check transfer fees and exchange rates?

Enter your transfer details on the provider's official website or app and review the final quote, fees, exchange rate, and amount the recipient should receive.

How long will my transfer take?

Delivery time depends on the provider, payment method, route, compliance checks, and other factors. Use the estimate shown by the provider for your transaction.

What if MTN MoMo is not available as a payout method?

Check another provider or ask the recipient about an acceptable alternative. Do not choose another payout method unless you understand how the recipient will receive the money.

Does this website process payments?

No. This is an independent personal information website. It does not collect money, process transfers, or confirm that a transaction has succeeded.

What should I do if my country is not listed?

Search for your country or choose “Other country”, then check sending eligibility directly with an official provider.
